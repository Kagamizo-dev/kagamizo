The research loop reads markets only, through the framework's own data layer with vendors declared by category: prices and technical indicators from Yahoo Finance, fundamentals from SEC EDGAR and Yahoo Finance, news from Yahoo Finance, macro series from FRED, and prediction-market prices from Polymarket. Every vendor is a public API; the hosts read are listed below. The loop calls no broker.

The decision is one JSON file per ticker per trading day holding the signal, the final decision text, every analyst's report and the debate, so that the proposal that follows can show its reasons and a backtest can be audited. The option chain the proposal step reads comes from the brokerage's quote API: the listed expirations, each contract's bid and ask, and its delta, fetched separately when the chain carries no greeks.
