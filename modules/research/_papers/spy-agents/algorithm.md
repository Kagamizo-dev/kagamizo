A BUY decision becomes a long call, a SELL a long put, a HOLD no trade. The expiration is the nearest one listed that is at least the declared minimum and at most the declared maximum number of days out; with none in the window there is no trade. Among the calls (or puts) of that expiration whose ask, times the contract multiplier of one hundred, is within the trade cap and whose absolute delta is at least the declared minimum, the cheapest ask is chosen, ties to the larger absolute delta; with none fitting there is no trade. The quantity is one contract, and the worst case is the ask times one hundred.

The proposal is held to the risk rule over the limits and the day's state, and only an admissible one is sent to the venue's preflight, which prices it and places nothing. The proposal, the decision it came from and its rationale, the rule's reasons and the preflight's answer are written as one record. Placing is a separate, owner-only step that runs the rule again and refuses any strategy not at the live stage.
