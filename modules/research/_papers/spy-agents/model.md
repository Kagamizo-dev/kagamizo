The model is the framework's graph, run with the settings declared below and otherwise unchanged. Analysts (market, fundamentals, news, sentiment) each write a report from the vendors' data; a bull researcher and a bear researcher debate for the declared number of rounds; a trader drafts a plan; a risk discussion of the declared number of rounds argues it from aggressive, conservative and neutral positions; a portfolio manager decides. Two model tiers are used: a deep model for the managers and a quick one for the analysts, researchers and trader, both open-weight and reachable through one OpenAI-compatible endpoint. An output cap per call bounds what any one step may spend.

The signal is parsed from the final decision text as the last of BUY, SELL or HOLD that appears in it, with HOLD when none does. There is no numeric forecast, position size or confidence in the signal: the mapping from decision to trade is the rule in the next section, and the only free parameters of the strategy are that rule's.
