The limits are the whole of the risk policy and they are public: a seed, the most one trade may cost, one open position, a daily stop and a daily take-profit, and the closed list of trade kinds allowed, each a kind whose worst case is its cost. A long call or put bought for a premium within the trade cap can lose that premium and no more; the rule admits nothing else. The numbers are small on purpose: at the research stage the strategy is being judged, not funded, and the limits are the owner's to raise once there is evidence.
