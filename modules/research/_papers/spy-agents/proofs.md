The risk rule and the stage gate are stated in Lean and proven there, and the proofs run as checks of the repository. The theorems below are extracted from the Lean sources with their docstrings and full statements. `admissible` is the rule every proposal is held to; the theorems say what it guarantees whatever the agents decide: a long option loses at most its premium, which is at most the trade cap; a day that admitted an order and then lost it all stays under the stop plus one cap; a stopped day, a day at its take-profit or a full book admit nothing. The declared limits are decided sane in the same check.

`wellStaged` is the stage gate: a strategy at the paper stage has a recorded backtest that met the thresholds, and a live one also has enough recorded paper days and the owner's dated decision. The papers' own redaction rule is proven in the same way and listed last: this document was rendered from a list of facts that Lean has checked contains only fields on a declared allowlist.
