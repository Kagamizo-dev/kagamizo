The limits are the whole of the risk policy and they are public: a seed, the most one trade may cost, the most one event-contract order may cost, one open position, a daily stop and a daily take-profit, and the closed list of trade kinds allowed, each a kind whose worst case is known when it is entered. A bought event contract settles at one or zero and loses at most its price, so an order of price times quantity within the event-contract cap is within the trade cap by the sanity theorem over the declared values. The numbers are small on purpose: at the research stage the strategy is being judged, not funded, and the limits are the owner's to raise once there is evidence.
