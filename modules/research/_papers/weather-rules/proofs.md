The risk rule and the stage gate are stated in Lean and proven there; the proofs run as checks of the repository, so a change that broke a guarantee would fail to build. The theorems below are extracted from the Lean sources with their docstrings and full statements. `admissible` is the rule every proposal is held to; the theorems say what holding to it guarantees whatever the strategy proposes: an order loses at most the trade cap, a day that admitted an order and then lost everything stays under the stop plus one cap, and a stopped day, a day at its take-profit or a full book admit nothing. The declared limits are rendered after the rule and decided sane in the same check: the caps and the stop never exceed the seed, and the event-contract cap never exceeds the trade cap, so an order within it is within the rule.

`wellStaged` is the stage gate: a strategy at the paper stage has a recorded backtest that met the thresholds, and a live one also has enough recorded paper days and the owner's dated decision. The papers' own redaction rule is proven in the same way, and its theorems are listed last: this document was rendered from a list of facts that Lean has checked contains only fields on a declared allowlist.
