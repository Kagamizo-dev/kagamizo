Once a day, the engine reads every unresolved event in the declared category, and for every open outcome that is tradeable it parses the question, computes the probability from the day's data, and records a candidate for each of the outcome's contracts (YES and NO) that has an ask strictly between zero and one, with its probability, ask and edge. Everything it skips is recorded with the reason: a resolved or halted event, an outcome not open, a question shape the rules do not handle or that the strategy does not declare, missing data, no usable ask.

Among the candidates at or above the declared minimum edge, the one with the greatest edge is proposed (ties to the cheaper ask), as a BUY of whole contracts: the quantity is the least of the strategy's most contracts per proposal, the event-contract cap divided by the ask, and the trade cap divided by the ask, rounded down; a quantity below one is no proposal. The proposal's worst case is its cost, price times quantity.

The proposal is then held to the risk rule over the limits and the day's state: the kind must be allowed, the worst case within the trade cap, the book below its limit, the day below its stop and below its take-profit. Only an admissible proposal is sent to the venue's preflight, which prices the order and places nothing. The proposal, the full list of candidates and skips, the rule's reasons and the preflight's answer are written as one record. Placing is a separate, owner-only step that runs the same rule again and refuses any strategy that is not at the live stage.
